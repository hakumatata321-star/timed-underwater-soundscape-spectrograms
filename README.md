# Sanctuary Soundscape Clock

A derived dataset of 1,600 mel-spectrogram images (60 x 96) of 30-second underwater recordings from 160 hydrophone deployments at 28 sites in eight U.S. National Marine Sanctuaries (Stellwagen Bank, Channel Islands, Florida Keys, Gray's Reef, Monterey Bay, Hawaiian Islands Humpback Whale, Olympic Coast, Papahanaumokuakea), recorded between March 2019 and June 2022. Each recording is labelled with the local solar hour and the day of year read from the recorder clock.

Source recordings: NOAA Office of National Marine Sanctuaries and U.S. Navy, Sanctuary Soundscape Monitoring Project (SanctSound), NOAA National Centers for Environmental Information, https://doi.org/10.25921/saca-sp25 (U.S. Government works, public domain).
